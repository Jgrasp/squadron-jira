# Changelog

## 0.3.0 · 2026-11-16

- The board and the list follow the columns of your Jira board, in its order, with the statuses each column holds
- Drag a column header to move it; Jira order puts them back
- The filters on the left of the board fold away and come back with the button next to the search

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-16

- The Jira logo on the extension and on the Jira button of each agent
- Board: the open sprint in a column per status, with filters by project, assignee, status, type, priority and epic
- Launch agent opens the New agent window filled by Haiku from the ticket, with the fields it reads to check or uncheck
- The ticket shows its fields beside its description when the panel is wide, and its comments can be searched and sorted

## 0.1.1 · 2026-11-16

- The API token field links to the Atlassian page that creates it, and says to pick the token without scopes

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-13

- The open sprint of the Jira projects of a Squadron project in a panel next to the agent
- A ticket goes into your message, or Haiku turns it into an instruction
- Attachments open in their app
- Agents get jira_ticket, jira_sprint and jira_search
